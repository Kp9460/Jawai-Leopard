JAWAI SAFARI FAQ

Before you book.

Planning a Jawai leopard safari? Here are a few practical details to help you choose an experience.

Are leopard sightings guaranteed on a Jawai safari?

No. Leopards are wild animals and sightings cannot be guaranteed. Your guide will look for wildlife while respecting the animals and their habitat.

How long are the Jawai safari experiences?

The sunrise and sunset safaris are listed as 2–3 hours. The full-day experience is a longer outing; confirm its schedule and inclusions with the team.

How do I confirm a safari booking?

Send your preferred date and safari details through the booking form to WhatsApp. The request is an enquiry; the team confirms availability and the final total in its reply.

Are the prices on the website final?

The listed rates are guide prices. Check availability, inclusions and the complete total with the team before making any payment.
