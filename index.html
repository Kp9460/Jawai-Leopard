ABOUT JAWAI

Where the wild feels at home.

Jawai is one of Rajasthan's most unique wilderness destinations. Here, magnificent leopards live freely among dramatic granite hills and ancient caves.

Unlike traditional wildlife parks, Jawai offers a completely different safari experience — open landscapes, local culture and unforgettable encounters with nature.
